Brad White's apps

Every app Brad White publishes, in one F-Droid repository. Scan once; the apps then install and update from F-Droid, Droid-ify or the Sidephone's App Library.

Add the repository

  1. In F-Droid, go to Settings, Repositories, tap +, then Scan QR code. On the Sidephone, open App Library and tap its QR code button.
  2. Scan the code above. Adding the repository installs nothing on its own.
  3. Find the app you want and tap Install. Updates arrive the same way.

On the phone itself, where the code can't be scanned: add the address below by hand and paste the fingerprint.

The apps

  • Slickrock Station

    Sets up RetroArch for you: a game library by system with box art, and controls drawn on the controller in your hands, whether keys, a keypad tile, a gamepad or the touchscreen.

    Download the APK directly
  • RetroArch for Slickrock

    The RetroArch build Slickrock Station launches: every press reaches the game the moment you make it. Installs beside the stock RetroArch and keeps its own settings. GPL; the patches are published here.

    Download the APK directly
  • Tyle For the Sidephone SP-01

    Per-tile settings for the keypad tiles: keyboard, Bluetooth, brightness and an app to open when a tile snaps on. Turn on "Tyle keys" under Accessibility after installing.

    Download the APK directly
  • Relay On the phone that keeps your SIM and Google Messages

    Forwards every text, SMS, MMS and RCS, groups and full-size pictures included, to your Text Relay inbox, along with texts sent from this phone and, when asked, its whole history. Replies go back out through Messages from that number. Leave it on Wi-Fi and a charger.

    Download the APK directly
  • Texts On the phone you carry

    Reads and answers every conversation the relay phone receives, with a notification for each one as texts arrive. Works with no Google services, push app or browser.

    Download the APK directly

Setting up takes one scan: open Relay on the phone with the SIM and it shows a code; tap Scan code in Texts on your other phone. The two phones get their own private inbox, encrypted on the phones, with no account or password. To add another phone or a browser, tap Link a device in Relay. Reinstalled on the same phone, either app gets back in by itself. A direct download installs the same build F-Droid serves; Android asks once to allow installs from your browser, and on phones with Google Play, Play Protect may ask you to confirm with More details, Install anyway.
