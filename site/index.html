Brad White's apps

These are the Android apps I have built. Add this repository to F-Droid once and you can install all of them and get their updates from there.

Adding the repository

  1. In F-Droid, go to Settings, then Repositories, tap + and choose Scan QR code. Most other clients have a QR button that does the same thing.
  2. Scan the code above. This only adds the repository. Nothing gets installed yet.
  3. Find the app you want and tap Install. Updates come through the same way.

The apps

  • Slickrock Station

    A game launcher that sets up RetroArch for you, so you can pick a game and play. I made it for phones with physical keys, Android devices with a controller connected, and touchscreens.

    Download the APK
  • RetroArch for Slickrock

    The RetroArch build Slickrock Station runs. I patched its input so quick presses always register, and it installs next to regular RetroArch without touching its settings. RetroArch is GPL, so my patches are published here.

    Download the APK
  • Tyle For the Sidephone

    Gives each keypad tile its own setup. Snap a tile on and Tyle switches the keyboard, Bluetooth and brightness, and can open an app for it. After you install it, turn on "Tyle keys" in Accessibility settings.

    Download the APK
  • Twyn For the Sidephone

    A predictive keyboard for the Compact QWERTY tile. Press each two-letter key once and the word you meant is in the field by the time you finish it. It picks the word from what you wrote before, learns your words, and suggests the next one. Turn it on under Languages and input, then pick it for the tile in Tyle.

    Download the APK
  • Paigr Relay On the phone with your SIM

    Sends every text that phone gets, including RCS, group chats and pictures, to your Paigr inbox. Your replies go back out from your number. Keep it on Wi-Fi and plugged in.

    Download the APK
  • Paigr On the phone you carry

    Read and answer your texts on any Android phone, even one without Google services. You get a notification for each conversation, and you can react, quote a message and send pictures and files.

    Download the APK

To set up Paigr, open Paigr Relay on the phone with your SIM and it shows a code. Tap Scan code in Paigr on your other phone and scan it. That's it! There's no account or password, and your texts are encrypted on the phones, so the server can't read them. Both apps can connect through Tor with Orbot, and you can run the inbox server yourself. The source is at github.com/Bradleyw801/paigr. To add another phone or a browser, tap Link a device in Paigr Relay.

The direct downloads are the same builds F-Droid gets. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser. On phones with Google Play, Play Protect may ask too. Tap More details, then Install anyway.
